# North Star

A living document of goals, aspirations, and current focus areas. Both you and Claude write to this. Claude reads it at the start of meaningful work sessions and references it when making suggestions.

## Current Focus

_Which client projects, design work, or skills am I focused on right now?_

-

## Goals

### Short-term (This Quarter)

_Client deliveries, launches, retainers to land or finish._

-

### Medium-term (This Half)

_Skills to level up, a design system, a portfolio piece, a product of my own._

-

### Long-term (This Year+)

_The kind of studio, clients, and work I want._

-

## Aspirations

_What kind of developer and designer am I becoming?_

-

## Anti-goals

_What am I explicitly NOT optimizing for? For example: clients who do not respect scope, work that does not teach me anything, hours over outcomes._

-

Record when focus changes, with date and reason.
