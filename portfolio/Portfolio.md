## How This Works

- Each quarter note has: Craft Evidence, Impact & Deliverables, Technical Growth, Collaboration, Client Feedback
- Craft evidence links both the craft note in `portfolio/competencies/` and the work note
- Case studies hold the before/after: the brief, what you changed, and the measured result
- Backlinks from quarterly notes accumulate on craft notes and work notes automatically
